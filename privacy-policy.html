Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 17, 2026
Operator: Poor Dude Holdings LLC (Wyoming), through its operating subsidiaries
Application: Family Rights App — case-record keeping for parents navigating child-welfare and termination-of-parental-rights (TPR) proceedings

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, hashed password, account creation timestamp.
  • Application data: case records (case title, jurisdiction, stage), parties (names and roles of family members, caseworkers, attorneys, judges), case timeline events, documents you upload (court filings, correspondence, evaluations) and the text extracted from them via OCR, issue flags generated from your documents, and your coalition-matching opt-in choice.
  • Payment data: subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store card numbers; we store only the Google Play purchase token and the resulting entitlement status used to unlock premium features.
  • Telemetry: error traces and crash reports (personally identifiable information is stripped on-device before transmission), plus server-side request logs used for fraud prevention and performance debugging.
  • Sensitive categories: case material in this app can include protected health information (PHI) such as medical, mental-health, and drug-test records. We treat this data as PHI and apply the HIPAA-aligned controls described in Section 8.

2. Why we collect it

  • To deliver the contracted service.
  • To comply with applicable laws (HIPAA, state privacy laws, and similar requirements).
  • To prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To improve the product (telemetry only, never sold).

4. Who we share with

This application uses the following subprocessors:

  • Payment processing — Google Play Billing (subscription purchases). Google does not share card data with us.
  • Cloud / hosting / storage — Appwrite (database, file storage, and serverless functions).
  • OCR — Google Cloud Vision API, used to extract text from documents you upload so the app can analyze them. Document bytes are sent to Google Cloud Vision only to perform this extraction.
  • Crash reporting — Sentry (error and crash telemetry, with personally identifiable information stripped on-device before transmission).

We do not sell personal data to advertisers or data brokers, and we do not use your case data to train third-party AI models.

5. Your rights

  • Access — request a copy of your data.
  • Deletion — request account deletion (GDPR Art. 17, CCPA).
  • Correction — request fixes to inaccurate data.
  • Portability — export your data in JSON.
  • Opt-out — unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time (one-click in every email).

Submit requests to malcolmgoodmen@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days (CCPA) / 1 month (GDPR).

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (US) / 16 (EU). Contact us if a minor's data was submitted in error and we will delete it. Case records maintained by a parent about their own proceedings may reference their children; that information is provided by and under the control of the parent account holder.

7. International transfers

Data may be processed in the United States. EU/UK users: we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework / UK Extension and standard contractual clauses where required.

8. Security

  • TLS 1.2+ in transit
  • Encryption at rest for sensitive fields (PHI, financial, credentials)
  • Role-based access control
  • Quarterly secret rotation
  • Audit logging of all sensitive operations
  • Annual security reviews

9. Account deletion

You can delete your account and its data from within the app (Settings → Delete account) or by emailing malcolmgoodmen@gmail.com. Deletion completes within 30 days, subject to the retention minimums in Section 3.
